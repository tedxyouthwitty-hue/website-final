This edition's theme

Metamorphosis

Every idea worth sharing starts the same way — as something unfinished. A question without an answer. A habit someone decided to break. A student who stopped accepting things as they were and started asking what they could become.

Metamorphosis is that in-between moment made visible: the discomfort of outgrowing an old shape before the new one has fully formed. It's biological before it's metaphorical — nothing transforms without first coming apart. We chose this theme because every speaker on our stage, and every student in our audience, is somewhere in that process right now: unlearning, rebuilding, becoming someone they weren't a year ago.

This isn't a theme about arriving. It's about the change itself — messy, ongoing, and worth talking about honestly.

The format

What is TEDx?

TED is a nonprofit devoted to spreading ideas, usually in the form of short, powerful talks. TEDx events are independently organized by local communities and licensed directly by TED, following the same format, spirit, and standards as the flagship TED conference — just built by and for the people closest to the ground.

The "x" stands for an independently organized event: TEDx events are produced under license, without TED's central organization directly involved in the content or curation, which means each one carries the voice and priorities of the community that built it.

Our event

About TEDxWittyIntlSchoolYouth

This is the first edition of TEDxWittyIntlSchoolYouth — a youth-led TEDx event bringing student voices, original ideas, and honest conversation to a single stage. Organized entirely by students, for students, this event exists to prove that meaningful ideas don't wait for a certain age or title to be worth hearing.

As a first edition, this is the starting point of something we intend to build year after year: a platform where young people are trusted to speak seriously about the things that matter to them.
